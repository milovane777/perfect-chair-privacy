Privacy Policy — Perfect Chair

App: Perfect Chair (com.perfectchair.perfectChair) · Last updated: 4 November 2026

Perfect Chair does not collect any data. It has no server, no account, and no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

None. The developer receives no information about you or your use of the app — not your name, not your clients' records, not usage statistics, not crash reports, not an advertising identifier.

On Android, the app's manifest explicitly removes the INTERNET permission, so the app is technically incapable of sending data anywhere. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking components.

2. Data stored on your device

The information you enter is written to the app's private storage area on your device only:

  • Client records (name, contact note, hair condition, allergy and patch-test notes you choose to enter)
  • Service records (formulas, processing times, prices, memos)
  • Photos you take or attach
  • Drawings, diagrams, tags, and goal records
  • App settings, including your language choice

Other apps cannot read this storage. The developer cannot read it either.

Client information is your responsibility

If you record details about other people, you are the data controller for that information under laws such as the GDPR or Korea's PIPA. Obtain their consent where your local law requires it, and keep your device locked.

3. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you take a before/after photo. The image is saved into the app's private storage.
  • Photo library — only when you pick an existing photo to attach. A copy is saved into the app's private storage; your library is not scanned or indexed.
  • Notifications — only to ring a local timer you started. Notifications are scheduled by your device; nothing is sent over a network.

You may decline any of these. The rest of the app continues to work.

4. Sharing and export

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to send it. When you tap share or export, your device's own share sheet opens and you pick the destination — a message, a file, another app. Once data leaves Perfect Chair this way, the receiving app's privacy policy applies, not this one.

5. Backups

When you create a backup, the file is written where you choose. Keep it somewhere safe: it contains your records in full.

On Android, a limited portion of app data may be included in the system backup your device makes to your own Google account, under Google's terms. Photos are deliberately excluded from that system backup. On iOS, app data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, under Apple's terms. In both cases the backup belongs to you and the developer has no access to it.

6. Children

Perfect Chair is a professional tool for hairdressers and is not directed at children.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There is nothing stored elsewhere for us to delete, and no account to close. Backup files you exported yourself are yours to delete.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, material changes are unlikely.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: milovane7777@gmail.com

없습니다. 개발자는 이용자나 앱 사용에 관한 어떤 정보도 받지 않습니다 — 이름도, 손님 기록도, 사용 통계도, 오류 보고도, 광고 식별자도 받지 않습니다.

Android 에서는 앱 설정에서 INTERNET 권한 자체를 제거했기 때문에, 앱이 데이터를 어디로든 보내는 것이 기술적으로 불가능합니다. 분석·광고·추적 구성요소도 들어 있지 않습니다.

  • 손님 기록(이름, 연락 메모, 모발 상태, 직접 적으신 알레르기·패치테스트 메모)

  • 마킹, 순서도, 태그, 목표 기록
  • 언어 설정을 포함한 앱 설정

다른 앱은 이 저장 공간을 읽을 수 없습니다. 개발자도 읽을 수 없습니다.

손님 정보는 이용자의 책임입니다

다른 사람에 관한 정보를 기록하시는 경우, 개인정보 보호법(PIPA)이나 GDPR 같은 법에서 말하는 개인정보처리자는 이용자 본인입니다. 법이 요구하는 경우 당사자의 동의를 받으시고, 기기 잠금을 꼭 켜 두십시오.

Android 에서는 앱 데이터의 일부가 기기가 이용자 본인의 Google 계정으로 만드는 시스템 백업에 포함될 수 있으며, 이는 Google 의 약관을 따릅니다. 사진은 그 시스템 백업에서 의도적으로 제외했습니다. iOS 에서는 앱 데이터가 이용자 본인의 iCloud 또는 컴퓨터 백업에 포함될 수 있으며, 이는 Apple 의 약관을 따릅니다. 두 경우 모두 그 백업은 이용자의 것이고 개발자는 접근할 수 없습니다.
